How to use

Night routine

  1. Wash your face with a gentle cleanser and lukewarm water.
  2. Pat dry with a clean towel. Don't rub.
  3. Take a small amount of cream and spread it evenly over the face and neck, about 30 minutes before sleep.
  4. Wash your face the next morning.

Day routine

  1. Cleanse and pat dry.
  2. Apply a thin, even layer as directed on the pack.
  3. Finish with sunscreen (SPF 30 or higher). Sun exposure can make marks look darker again.

What results to expect

Skin changes slowly, so judge the cream over several weeks of regular use, not a few days. Results differ from person to person and depend on how old the marks are, how regularly you use it and whether you protect your skin from the sun. Taking a photo in the same light every two weeks makes progress easier to see.

Safety and patch test

  • Patch test behind the ear or on the inner arm and check after 24 hours.
  • Keep away from the eyes. If the cream gets in them, rinse well with water.
  • Do not use on broken, sunburnt or very irritated skin.
  • Stop if you notice burning, itching, swelling or redness that doesn't settle.
  • Ask a doctor or dermatologist first if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, use prescription skin treatments or have a skin condition.
  • This is a cosmetic product. It is not meant to diagnose or treat any medical condition.
